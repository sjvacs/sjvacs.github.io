The San Joaquin Valley Section of the American Chemical Society is a local professional organization serving chemists and professionals in chemistry-related fields in Fresno, CA, and surrounding areas. Members come from both the chemical industry and academia and include both those employed in the field and students studying in the field. Current membership is approximately 200 members.
